Business model · draft for discussion

Teachers fund almost none of it. Systems fund the rest.

01 · Individual
Tended+ subscription

$5.99/mo or $49/yr. Deliberately modest: history, comparisons, exports and audio. Assume low single-digit conversion — this is retention and credibility revenue, not the business.

Small, predictable, ethical
02 · Commerce
Journal, HELD, bulk orders

The app is the best storefront the Let It Out Journal will ever have: a teacher who just logged three hard weeks is the exact reader for it. HELD and The Shift enroll from the same moment, and buildings buy journals in bulk.

Highest margin today
03 · Institutional
Consultancy & The Shift

Districts pay for culture work, not for teacher data. Tended sells the diagnosis-and-change engagement; the app supplies free, public, ZIP-level evidence that the problem is structural. Sponsored Tended+ for a whole district is a clean line item.

Where the scale is
Never for sale
  • Individual check-ins, notes or trends — to anyone, at any price
  • School-level or building-level breakdowns
  • Ad inventory, or anything resembling it
  • ZIP data behind a paywall — the briefs are public and free
